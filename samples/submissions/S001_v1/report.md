# Bike Rentals Report

## Introduction
My research question is: how do daily bike rentals vary across the year, and what is a typical day?

## Data
I loaded the dataset with pandas using read_csv. The dataset has 731 rows and 16 columns.

## Summary statistics
The mean of daily rentals is 4504 and the median is 4548. The mean is lower than the median
because a small number of days with very low rentals (bad weather) pull the mean down.

## Histogram
Figure 1 is a histogram of daily rentals. The x axis is labelled "Daily rentals", the y axis is
labelled "Number of days" and the title is "Distribution of daily bike rentals".

## Limitations
One limitation is that the dataset only covers two years in a single city, so results may not generalise.
